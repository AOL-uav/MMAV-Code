STEP 1  static margin in millimetres
   MAC (the 'Cref' line on the AVL screen)      = 47.406 mm
   margin 0.10 x MAC                          = 4.741 mm
STEP 2  CG = neutral point - margin
   41.713 - 4.741                        = 36.972 mm behind the root leading edge
STEP 3  the glider before any nose weight (foam sheet + printed rail)
   foam sheet     6.651 g   (sheet area x foamboard areal mass, ASSUMED 300 g/m2)
   printed rail   1.911 g
   together M0    8.563 g, balancing at cg0 = 43.95 mm
STEP 4  how much weight at the nose hole to move the balance point back to the target
   target CG      36.97 mm;  nose weight position (rail hole) -12.5 mm
   weight = M0 x (target - cg0) / (position - target)
          = 8.563 x (36.97 - 43.95) / (-12.5 - 36.97)
          = 1.207 g
   all-up mass    9.770 g   (nose weight is 12.4 % of it)
   wing loading   5.46 N/m2
STEP 5  first-swoop depth estimate (formula, MODEL)
   depth = 3 x (W/S) / (rho x g x CL) = 3 x 5.46 / (1.225 x 9.80665 x 0.3) = 4.55 m
