Tumble simulation, Li, Goodwill, Wang & Ristroph 2022 model

Not a flight result. A 2-D flat-plate quasi-steady model (JFM 937 A6, eqs 4.4-4.15 and 5.1-5.7, all constants CITED) applied to 3-D swept, tailed, low-aspect-ratio gliders as a strip. The tail bend (reflex) is not in the model and is not represented. Step A was an implementation/consistency check (passed for the variant declared in advance; the generic-inertia variant fails the same rule), not a validation; the dive edge near l_CM/l = 0.30 follows from the cited centre-of-pressure coefficient. This model is not usable for setting a CG. Outcomes near the mark and at +3 mm are start-dependent and change with the strip definition. See the README, including the drop-window results below.
