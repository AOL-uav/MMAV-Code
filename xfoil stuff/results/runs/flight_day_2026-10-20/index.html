1. B45 foamboard 400 mm (control)

Flown 2026-10-19 with centre flaps. It glides, with phugoid or possibly stall-porpoise swoops (Kai's description OBSERVED-from-video). Flaps: about 10 mm chord x 50 mm span, about 3 cm from the centreline, about 10 deg trailing edge UP (Kai's reading, not measured ASSUMED).

Files: spec FLAT_TEST.md; model README.md, tables.md, results.json; build files B45_span400/: half-wing template, fin template, templates PDF, stick.stl, drawing.svg, geometry.json; test page flat_test_v2/index.html.

Model facts: neutral point 70.24 mm behind the root LE (flap 0), 70.30 at 10 deg MODEL; MAC 92.2 mm GEOMETRY. The model says the flight works only for CG about 63-68 mm MODEL (trim CL 0.2-0.9 rule, tables.md verdict). Behind about 70.3 mm it is unstable. The model has no stall in it and sees no swoop dynamics.

Plan

  1. First, as flown, no changes: balance on the knife edge, record the CG and the mass (fields below). Throw 5 times and note what the swoops look like.
  2. Then ONE change aimed at the swoops (not both):
    • Option F: bend the flaps down from about 10 to about 6-7 deg up. Less flap means a lower trim CL and a faster glide.
    • Option W: add nose weight to move the CG 1-2 mm forward. Same effect on trim CL, flaps left alone. Moment balance (ARITHMETIC): m = M d / (CG - x_hole - d). With M 17.5 g (ASSUMED), CG 66 mm, a hole at -37.5 mm (the stick's furthest ballast hole, GEOMETRY from geometry.json): d = 1 mm needs about 0.17 g, d = 2 mm about 0.34 g. Use the real CG and mass you measure; this is only a sizing hint.

Trim CL and trim speed against CG and flap (nominal flap location) MODEL

Source: b45_centre_flaps/results.json (cases and CG sweep) and tables.md. The model is linear in flap angle (dCm0/dflap 0.00206 per deg at the nominal location), so CL_trim = Cm_np / SM with Cm_np = 0.00187 per deg of flap, SM = (Xnp - CG) / MAC. Flap 10 deg reproduces the file's own CG sweep (check below); 5 and 7 deg are the same relation, not separate runs. Speed at 17.50 g and wing area 35,556 mm2 (ASSUMED mass, GEOMETRY area), air density 1.225 kg/m3: use your measured mass to rescale (speed goes as the square root of mass). The alternate flap location (y 5-55 mm) gives CL about 15% lower. The flap deflection here is the real fold angle, which on foamboard is a crude reflex.

How to make the bend

  • The tail is the fan behind the hinge line (orange line on the drawing). Bend it so the trailing edge goes UP (tail set nose-down relative to the wing). One sheet, one bend.
  • Template note page text: 'Score the top face, bend up to the gauge, glue the crease.' Lay the wing along the long edge of the gauge wedge; the tail follows the slanted edge. The 6 deg wedge is on template page 2 (foam page 2, printed page 2). Other angles (5 deg for the alternate): field_pack.pdf page 4.
  • Check the 100 mm scale bar on any printed gauge before using it. The wrist line and body edge on the drawing are markers only; do not cut or crease there.

Primary 6 deg is the build setting on the page (10% margin; the 8% floor is JUDGEMENT and the real lift point may sit 0-5% MAC forward of the model, so 8% could be 3-5% in practice). The model's tail effect is unvalidated (inviscid AVL, small tail in the wake). If 6 deg still tumbles, do not add a second change blindly: move the CG, not the bend, next, and record the CG.

Print settings (only what the project states)

  • Print sheet.stl flat on the bed. Plate 0.6 mm = 3 layers of 0.2 mm (rigid_glider/FLAT_TEST.md, bird_outline.py docstring).
  • The sheet is three overlapping closed shells: main sheet, a scored hinge strip 0.4 mm thick (two layers) and 1.0 mm wide, and the fan tail (bird_outline_v2.py HINGE_V2_MM, bird_outline.py HINGE_WIDTH_MM). Slicers should merge them; look at the preview to confirm the hinge strip is there.
  • Material: PLA density 1.24 g/cm3 (ASSUMED) is behind the mass numbers. A 0.6 mm plate is one to two 0.4 mm perimeters, so use 100% infill or all perimeters (the project's advice for the Zanonia plate, same thickness). Nozzle, speed, temperature: UNKNOWN, not stated by the project.
  • Hinge strength: the printed hinge strip is screening arithmetic only (page section 8); a crease bent more than once may fail. Bend once to the gauge.

4. Zanonia replica 155 mm (3D print, LW-PLA preferred)

Test card (written before any flight, rule fixed): TEST_CARD.md. Also RESULTS.md, results page, numbers.json, templates.pdf (cardstock templates). Files: zanonia_plate_155mm.stl, jigs 2, 4, 6 deg (more: 8, 10, 12 are in the same folder).

Higher loading than the foam original (1.7 N/m2) means faster flight and a deeper first swoop: launch a bit higher.

Build, in short (TEST_CARD section 3)

  1. Weigh the plate.
  2. Warm the outer tips (hot water about 60-70 degC, or a heat gun on low) and press the plate upside down on the 4 deg jig until cool; both tips must match.
  3. Add nose weight until it balances on the knife edge at the CG cross; weigh the finished glider; note any left-right twist and do not trim it out until the first flights.

'It works' rule (fixed 2026-10-19, TEST_CARD section 4)

  • Indoors or dead-calm air. Gentle, level, straight hand launch at shoulder height (about 1.5 m); 5 launches per setting.
  • Works = at the marked CG and some single jig curl between 2 and 12 deg, at least 4 of 5 launches give a steady glide: at most one swoop, then no repeated stall-dive cycle, no tumble, no nose-in, and a ground distance at least 4 times the launch height (about 6 m from 1.5 m). A steady turn counts; note its direction.
  • Curl search: start at 4 deg. Dives or no pull-up: re-bend at 6, then 8, 10, 12. Stalls or porpoises: re-bend at 2. Stop at the first curl that meets the rule, then fly 5 more there to confirm.
  • Does not work = no curl from 2 to 12 deg meets the rule at the marked CG. The model already said the marked CG is near neutral (margin 1% MAC, AVL DISAGREE verdict).
  • Side tests only after the verdict: CG 1 and 2 mm forward with more curl; two nose-down drops from about 2 m released at rest.
