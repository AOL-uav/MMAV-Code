Goal

Release nose-down at rest from the quad: pulls out unaided, how much height it takes, then a straight, steady glide. Nothing has been flown; all predictions are model values.

Procedure

  1. Servo test with propellers off; drop zone clear of people; keep clear of the quad.
  2. Steady hover; release only when the article hangs still; abort if it swings.
  3. Record the drop height against a fixed reference; film side-on with it in view.
  4. One change between drops.
  5. Hand-glide low over grass first as a trim check; log it as H.

Model limits

  • Force data: plates at Re 60,000 MEASURED; swift outlines are swept and tapered at Re 15,000-40,000 MODEL.
  • Pitch-only simulation from rest in still air: no yaw, roll, downwash or string.
  • Near-neutral CG unresolved: 423 of 707 cases flagged; 76 of 88 swift cases MODEL.
  • Tail-bend effect rests on an inviscid tail in the wake, Re near 10,000; unvalidated MODEL.
