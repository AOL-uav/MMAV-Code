CORRECTION AND JUMP FINDING (added after second review; from jumps.csv and coverage.csv, generated by rigid_glider/plate_te_jumps.py re-reading the existing polar files, no XFOIL)
- Every converging plate variant I checked has a single alpha step (0.5 deg) inside the trim band where cl and cm jump together and top transition
  (xtr_top) moves from ~1.0 to 0.04-0.35.  Of the 22 non-control runs meeting the 15-point floor, 15 have a largest single cl step >= 0.15
  (steps 0.16-0.45, cm steps -0.02 to -0.08, for those; one run, ppar_te1 Re60k N5, has a -0.26 drop instead); the other 7 have largest steps 0.05-0.14.
