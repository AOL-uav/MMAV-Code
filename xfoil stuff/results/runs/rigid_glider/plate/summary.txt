RESULT IN ONE LINE
XFOIL 6.99, run with the existing small-scale runner unchanged, returned 0 of 406 viscous points for the clean 4.3% plate
(14 polars: Re 15k-100k x Ncrit 5, 9) and 0 of 406 for the 4.3% plate with the 20%/10 deg lip. The 3.0% plate returned 26
scattered points (7 of 14 polars non-empty) and the 15%/8 lip 22 (5 of 14); none of these supports a coupled-model lookup.
Every plate cell in comparison_P07.csv is therefore "insufficient coverage". The plate-vs-NACA 0006 comparison the brief asked
for cannot be made from this method. No judgement on better/worse is made or possible.

DOES THE PLATE SHOW THE NOSE-UP MOMENT HUMP?
Not determinable (no viscous data).  For reference the NACA 0006 viscous section (pure_wing database, Ncrit 5 / 9) has a
peak nose-up cm of +0.013 to +0.015 (cl 0.16-0.27 at Ncrit 9; Re 30k Ncrit 5 at cl 0.23), cm(cl 0.2) up to +0.015 at Re 60-80k
Ncrit 9, i.e. the hump the pure-wing trims rely on; the NACA 0006 inviscid cm is only -0.001 to -0.002.  The plate's inviscid
cm is the same sign and size, so any hump on the plate would have to come from the viscous solution, which is what failed.

ASSUMPTIONS

  consulted.  The TE ellipse ends in a rounded tip (vertical tangent, radius h/10) closed at (1,0); XFOIL reports "Sharp trailing edge".
- Lip convention as in the existing study: pure rotation about x = 1-cf, no gap, 15%/8 and 20/10 on the 4.3% plate only.
  The 15% and 20% hinges (x = 0.85, 0.80) lie inside the TE ellipse (starts at x = 0.785 for 4.3%).
- The coupled model, AVL deck, section data lookup, forms (a)/(b)/(c), window definition and tiers are the existing ones unchanged.
  AVL decks use NACA 0006/0008 labels on zero-camber sections, so AVL itself is the same for plate and NACA.
- Clean 160-panel plate polars come from this run (ITER 400, alpha -4..10), whereas the NACA clean 160 tables come from the
  pure_wing database (its own ITER-200 schedule plus 0.25 deg fill).  Moot here; relevant if the plate polars are redone.

CONVERGENCE FLOOR AND SECTION_SUMMARY.CSV (added after review)
Floor: a viscous polar must have at least 15 of the 29 requested alpha points.  In section_summary.csv, rows below the floor have
cl_max, alpha_at_cl_max, cm_at_cl_*, peak_nose_up_cm and the lip-moment/retained-fraction fields blanked and below_floor = 1;
n_converged and the alpha coverage are kept.  No plate viscous row meets the floor, so every viscous row is blanked.
The "pass" column in polars/plate_polar_database.csv is a constant 1 copied from the existing database format; it is NOT a
quality flag (unconverged-looking points such as the non-monotone PLATE_30 ones carry pass = 1).  The database was not regenerated.

SECOND-REVIEW FINDING (record only; see plate_te_diag/diag_summary.txt, jumps.csv)
At Re 60k-100k, XFOIL on this plate is trailing-edge-treatment dependent (as-is closure: no solution; blunt base / sharp wedge: solutions), and every
converging variant checked shows a lift-and-moment jump of a single 0.5 deg step inside the trim band (cl +0.16 to +0.45 for 15 of 22 runs, cm -0.02 to -0.08,
xtr_top 1.0 -> 0.04-0.35) with post-jump viscous cl above the inviscid plate value (viscous/inviscid 1.1-2.0 at 3 deg).  This disagrees with the published smooth
polar and near-zero pre-stall quarter-chord moment (Ananda et al. 2015, Sections 3.1 and 3.4, text only).  The "MRCHUE convergence failed at 80/81 side 1" message
also appears in converging runs and the NACA control, so it is not a failure signature.  No plate CG window is available from this chain; the choice of any further
plate modelling is Kai's.
