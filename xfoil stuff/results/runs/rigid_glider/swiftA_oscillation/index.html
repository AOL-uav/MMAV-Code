2. What the model says, in plain language

  • As-flown case (73.0 mm, 6 deg) MODEL: pulls out after losing 6.93 m, then swoops with a period of about 3.02 s (first flight-path amplitude 42.2 deg, shrinking by a factor 0.58 per cycle). Class: persistent phugoid. Stall (angle of attack past the lift peak) happens in the first swoop only. Time until the flight path stays within 2 deg of its final value: 20.3 s.
  • So the model calls it mostly a phugoid (a long speed-for-height swoop with damping ratio 0.086, MODEL), started by the nose-down release, with one stall at the top of the first swoop. In the model it is a transient that settles to within 2 deg after about 20 s and there is no sustained limit cycle at the flown setting. If the real oscillation lasted much longer than that or did not decay, the model is missing something (for example unsteady stall, hinge flutter or a larger real CG/bend error).
  • Predicted phugoid period at the as-flown case: linear 3.22 s (range 3.11-4.32 s over the sensitivity cases), in the simulation 3.16 s, textbook pi x sqrt(2) x V / g = 2.49 s at trim V 5.5 m/s. The textbook formula is about 23% short of the model because it ignores drag. Short period 0.24 s (too fast to see by eye).
  • Agreement with the flight: (a) pass, (b) fail, (c) fail. The model does not reproduce 'less bend gives a nose dive' at CG 73 mm: in the base model it keeps gliding down to about 1 deg of bend. Section 6 shows which combination of weaker bend and zero-lift moment offset would be needed for a dive at 3-4 deg.
  • Smooth region: 23 of 64 cells, forward CG and small bend (more static margin, less trim lift). 14 cells are smooth in every one of the 8 model variants. Stall porpoising sits at the aft-CG, large-bend corner (22 cells), where the trim is close to the lift peak.

8. Definitions and files

  • Pulls out: the flight path becomes shallower than -20 deg after release and the glider does not turn through a full rotation. Height lost = height at that moment.
  • Glide (part a): pulls out, never returns steeper than -80 deg, mean flight path over the last 5 s is shallower than -45 deg.
  • Settled: the flight path stays within a 5 deg band over the last 5 s of the 30 s run.
  • Cycle: from one flight-path maximum after pull-out to the next (extrema need 0.5 deg of movement). Amplitude of a cycle = half of (its maximum minus its following minimum). Decay ratio per cycle = (amplitude of cycle n / amplitude of cycle 1) to the power 1/(n-1), n = up to 6 cycles.
  • Smooth: pulls out, settles, cycle-3 amplitude below 25 % of cycle 1, and no stall after the first cycle.
  • Stall porpoise: alpha (minus the bend's alpha shift) passes the plate table's first lift peak on 2 or more cycles after pull-out. A cycle counts as stalled if alpha passes the peak anywhere between its flight-path maximum and the next one (this includes the swoop top).
  • Persistent phugoid: a glide that is not smooth and not a porpoise. Note that it can still die out; check the decay ratio and settle time.
  • Dive: anything that fails the glide test.
