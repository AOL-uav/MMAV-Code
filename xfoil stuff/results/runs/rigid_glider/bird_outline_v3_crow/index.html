Crow-like outline: can the proportions meet every criterion?

Outline-only AVL model plus arithmetic. Nothing here is a flight result. Tags as in version 2: GEOMETRY, AVL, ARITHMETIC, JUDGEMENT, ASSUMED, CITED. AVL is inviscid and ignores the body; it says nothing about stall, Reynolds number, or whether a build pulls out. Aspect ratio is held at 5 (CITED: magpie 4.97, jackdaw about 6). Varied: tail length, tail spread, nose length ahead of the wing, hand sweep, and the tail edges folded up in place of the ventral fin. All numbers below are at the finer panelling (16 x 128 per half wing); screening used 10 x 80.

No outline in the search passes every criterion. Closest candidate: tail length 0.08, spread 0.48, nose 0.3, hand sweep 40, tail edges up 60 deg, bend 4. It fails: ballast 21% of all-up mass at printed size (limit 20%); tip loading 0.914 above v1 value 0.888.

Which criteria block

Ballast against static margin. 476 candidates fail only the ballast limit; the least ballast any of them reaches is 20.9% of the all-up mass (tail length 0.08, spread 0.48, nose 0.3, sweep 40, tail edges up 60 deg, bend 5; static margin 11.3%, screening values). 141 candidates meet ballast and every other criterion but fail the static-margin window; the largest margin any of them reaches is 8.0% against the 8% floor (tail length 0.16, spread 0.48, nose 0.3, sweep 40, tail edges up 60 deg, bend 2). The reason in the model: a margin of 8% or more needs a tail bend, the bend moves the trim CG forward of the neutral point, and the sheet's own CG is behind it, so the difference is ballast. Within the searched ranges the two limits meet at about an 8% margin and about 21% ballast. 4 candidates passed everything at the screening panelling; none survived the re-run at the finer panelling. The gap that remains (about one point of ballast, a few per cent of the tip-loading indicator) is smaller than the panelling spread in the table below, so read this as 'no outline passes in this search', not as a proof that none exists.

9000 candidates (5 tail lengths x 5 spreads x 4 sweeps x 3 yaw options x 5 nose lengths x 6 bends; the bends other than 0 and 5 come from the model's linearity in tail incidence, and the best candidates were re-run exactly).
