Steps

  1. Load the video (file box below). Set the frame rate it was filmed at.
  2. Press Set scale, click both ends of the reference, then type its known length in m.
  3. Optional: press Set level line and click two points on the horizon or ground to remove camera roll.
  4. Press Track. Step to the release frame, click the glider. After each click the video advances N frames. Click again at every step.
  5. Select a point in the list and tag it: release, lowest, level-off, glide start, glide end. Release is the first point and lowest is the deepest point unless you override them.
  6. Read the results, fill in the drop details, then export the CSVs.
Keys: , back one frame, . forward one frame, Enter or N forward N frames, Space play/pause, Z undo the last point. Clicking again on a frame that already has a point moves that point.
